About

About & Contact

Masjid Khadijah (رضي الله عنها) opened its doors in July 2025 at 802 Arcadia Street, Arcadia — centrally positioned between Muelmed and the Heart Hospital, on the doorstep of Hatfield and the Pretoria CBD, and roughly 2 km from the University of Pretoria. The masjid welcomes residents, students and travellers alike.

  • Daily five salaah with jamaat, and Jumu’ah
  • Fully equipped ladies’ prayer facilities
  • Afternoon maktab for boys and girls
  • Convenient for students and travellers on the N4
