    About

    About & Contact

    Masjid Khadijah (رضي الله عنها) is situated in the heart of Arcadia, Pretoria — on the doorstep of the Pretoria CBD and Hatfield, close to the University of Pretoria, Pretoria Heart Hospital, Muelmed Hospital and Pretoria Urology Hospital, and the many embassies of the surrounding area. It serves residents, students, professionals, healthcare workers, patients, families, diplomats, visitors and travellers alike.

    • Daily five salaah with jamaat, and Jumu’ah
    • Dedicated facilities for females
    • Full-time and part-time madrassah for boys and girls
    • A full-time Imam available for religious guidance
